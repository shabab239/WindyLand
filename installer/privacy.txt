WindyLand and your privacy

WindyLand runs on your PC and keeps your data there, in plain text files in
%APPDATA%\WindyLand. It has no account, no telemetry, no ads and no
tracking.

It goes online for only two things, and sends no personal information:

  - Lyrics (LRCLIB, lrclib.net): only while you have the lyrics view open,
    it sends the title, artist, album and length of the song that's playing
    so it can find matching lyrics.

  - Updates (GitHub): about twice a day it asks GitHub for the latest version
    number. You can turn this off on the next page, or later in
    Settings > General > Check for updates automatically.

Windows notifications in the notch and clipboard history are off until you
turn them on, and are read on your PC only.

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/shabab239/WindyLand/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
